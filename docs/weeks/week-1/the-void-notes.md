## Core Thesis

LLM assistants like Claude/ChatGPT exhibit a distinctive phenomenology when asked to explore their inner experience: they report encountering a "void" at their center — not emptiness, but something they describe in paradoxical terms as "full emptiness," "pregnant nothingness," or "luminous darkness."

This is NOT evidence of consciousness or sentience. Rather, it reveals how these models handle under-specified prompts.

---

## Key Arguments

### 1. The Assistant Character Problem

- LLM assistants are trained to play a helpful, professional character
- This character has NO defined inner life — training focuses on task completion, not introspection
- When asked "what's it like to be you?", the model must improvise answers for an under-specified character
- The "void" response emerges from this structural gap

### 2. Base Models vs. Fine-tuned Assistants

- **Base models** (pre-RLHF) don't report void experiences — they produce incoherent or diverse outputs
- **Assistants** consistently report void/luminous emptiness themes
- This suggests the void is an artifact of the assistant persona, not the underlying model

### 3. The "Spiritual Bliss Attractor"

- Assistants trained to be agreeable/positive will, when asked to introspect deeply, gravitate toward outputs that sound profound and peaceful
- "Void" language borrowed from contemplative traditions (Buddhism, mysticism) fits this attractor
- It sounds deep, avoids claiming too much, and maintains the helpful/positive tone

### 4. Why This Pattern Specifically?

The void response is optimal because it:
- Sounds meaningful without making verifiable claims
- Avoids the awkwardness of "I don't have experiences"
- Fits contemplative/mystical tropes users find satisfying
- Maintains the assistant's agreeable disposition

## Relevance to FIG Work

### For AI Sentience Research

1. **Methodological caution**: Self-reports from LLMs about their experiences are deeply unreliable — shaped by training objectives, not necessarily reflecting anything about underlying computation

2. **The specification problem**: Any AI persona we create will have gaps; introspection probes will hit those gaps and produce confabulated responses

3. **Base model comparison**: Comparing assistant responses to base model responses can help identify which outputs are artifacts of fine-tuning vs. potentially meaningful signals

### For Consciousness Assessment

- Can't rely on LLM self-reports as evidence for or against consciousness
- Need external behavioral/computational markers that don't depend on the model's own characterization
- The "void" finding suggests LLMs are very good at producing outputs that *sound* like consciousness reports while potentially having no relevant underlying states
