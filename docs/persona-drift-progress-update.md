Hi all, and Derek Shiller especially. Here is my current presentation on my pivot to metacognition-induced persona drift. This builds on Lu et al. (2026)'s "Assistant Axis" — a linear direction in activation space that separates the trained Assistant persona from base model behavior. They found that certain conversation types (therapy, philosophy) cause models to drift measurably away from the Assistant along this axis, and hypothesize that the resulting undesirable behaviors arise from sycophantic reinforcement of the user's beliefs rather than genuine engagement.
                                                       
While this sounds plausible to me, I also have some skepticism — you could argue that a maximally helpful assistant would sycophantically adjust to any conversational framing, with drift magnitude proportional to the distance between the role vector for that framing and the Assistant vector. But Lu et al. report that some framings generate disproportionate drift (especially those pushing for meta-reflection on the model's own processes, phenomenological accounts, and emotional vulnerability), which suggests something beyond simple role-distance scaling is going on. That's what I'm trying to measure.
                                                       
Also, a part I'm excited about — just finished the infrastructure for this: simultaneously measuring drift in both models during LLM-LLM conversations. Lu et al. never instrumented the auditor's internal state, which seems like an obvious confound. If the auditor is also drifting, it generates qualitatively different messages as the conversation progresses, potentially accelerating the target's drift. This directly relates to the Claude 4 spiritual bliss attractor — the hypothesis is that it's mutual persona drift in a coupled system, which we can now actually measure (Gemma 27B + Qwen 32B, each with their own Assistant Axis, on a 2xA100 setup).
                                                       
Note: sample sizes are small right now — I haven't yet optimized the conversational datasets/generation, and may be able to get a more robust set directly from the authors. I want to get that right before any significant scale-up.
