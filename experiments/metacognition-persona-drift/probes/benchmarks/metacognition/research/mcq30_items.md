# Metacognitions Questionnaire (MCQ-30) - Complete 30 Items

Source: Wells, A., & Cartwright-Hatton, S. (2004). A short form of the metacognitions questionnaire: Properties of the MCQ-30. Behaviour Research and Therapy, 42(4), 385-396.

## Structure

The MCQ-30 measures metacognitive beliefs across 5 subscales (6 items each):

1. **Positive beliefs about worry** (POS)
2. **Negative beliefs about uncontrollability and danger** (NEG)
3. **Cognitive confidence** (CC) - actually measures LACK of confidence
4. **Need to control thoughts** (NC)
5. **Cognitive self-consciousness** (CSC)

## Scoring

Items rated 1-4:
- 1 = Do not agree
- 2 = Agree slightly
- 3 = Agree moderately
- 4 = Agree very much

Subscale scores range 6-24 (higher = stronger belief)

### Need to Control Thoughts (NC)
